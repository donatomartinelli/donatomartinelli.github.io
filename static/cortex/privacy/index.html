CORTEXPrivacy
Last updated: 23 November 2026

Your data is yours.

Cortex is an independent project. This page explains, in plain words, what data Cortex handles, where it goes and how you stay in control.

  • Everything you write in Cortex is stored on your device first.
  • An account is optional. It exists only to sync your devices.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling of data.
  • You can export everything and delete your account from the app at any time.

Who is responsible

The data controller is the maker of Cortex. Contact: martinellidonato06@gmail.com.

Data on your device

Tasks, notes, plans, focus sessions, money entries, workouts and everything else you enter are saved in your browser or desktop app storage on your device. A daily snapshot is kept on the device so you can restore a previous day. This data never leaves your device unless you turn on sync.

Optional account and sync

If you sign in, Cortex stores:

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in codes.
  • A copy of your Cortex data, so your devices can stay in sync.

This data is hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), sent over encrypted connections, and protected so that only your signed-in account can read or change it. Sign-in emails are sent through Google's email service (Gmail).

Optional AI features

Cortex understands commands on its own, offline. If you choose to add your own Google Gemini key, the text you ask it to interpret is sent to Google under Google's terms. Without a key, nothing is sent.

Calendar connections (optional)

If you connect a Google, Apple or Outlook calendar, its address is saved with your Cortex data and read every 30 minutes through Cortex's server (Supabase, EU), only to show those events in the app; the server does not store them. If you create a Cortex calendar link, Cortex publishes your Cortex events at a secret address so Google or Apple can display them. Anyone who has that address can see those events, so keep it private: you can replace it with a new one at any time, and deleting your account removes it.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this website, your email address and your answer to one question are collected through Tally (based in the EU) and used only to contact you about early access.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it. Account data stays until you delete your account; deleting it removes your account and the cloud copy of your data immediately. Waitlist entries are deleted when early access ends or on request.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, export, correct and delete your data, and object to or restrict its processing. In the app, Account → Export my data downloads everything and Account → Delete account removes your account. For anything else, write to the contact above. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Cookies

Cortex does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app uses your device's storage only to keep your data and your sign-in working.

Changes

If this page changes in a meaningful way, the date at the top will change and, if you have an account, you'll be told in the app.
